# Product

Mesurado is a curated, offline-first learning companion for Liberian and West African students (WASSCE
preparation), their teachers and school libraries. It pairs a trusted multimedia encyclopedia with a tutor that
answers only from its own sources and shows the citation. Entry: Kiroween 2026, Resurrection track.

## Users and goal
Students with little or no connectivity (about 70% of Liberians are offline). With the device in airplane mode a
student can look up a topic, explore the map and timeline, take a quiz, and ask a question answered only from the
content pack, with the source passage shown.

## Demo platform
The demo and primary target is a low-end Android phone running Chrome, with a laptop as backup. Design for a small touch screen first.

## Features in scope
Articles and images, hybrid keyword and semantic search, timeline, offline map, quizzes from passages, Ask mode
(retrieval-only, Tier 1). Stretch: dictionary. Pack v1: Liberian and African history plus geography; science and
mathematics only if the plan's gate M2 passes on time.

## Out of scope
Accounts, cloud sync, teacher dashboard, voice, native Android app, live updates, on-device generative model
(cut 2026-11-16; documented as future work), any content that is not openly licensed.

## Naming
Never write "Encarta" in code, UI, repo text or demo (Microsoft trademark). The write-up may mention it once as a
historical reference. Product name: Mesurado.

## Tone
Plain, respectful, short sentences. Students may read English as a second register: avoid idioms.
